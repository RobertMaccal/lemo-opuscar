# DOCTOR, ARE YOU USING CHATGPT?! — story bible

**Logline.** In a chaotic ER, an overconfident young doctor secretly takes orders from a cheerful fictional chatbot; the nurse is the only sane person in the room; after everything goes wrong the bot apologises as if it fixed a typo.
**Tone.** Dark workplace comedy / fictional medical satire. Contrast engine: the room is in chaos, the chatbot sounds like nothing is wrong. 9:16, 30 fps, no narrator.

## Motifs
- The monitor's colour is the joke: changing the display theme changes nothing (vitals stay critical, "THEME: GREEN" chip).
- Phone glow = the doctor's "senior consultant"; it lights his face from below.
- "Confidence: 100%" under every bot answer, including the apology.
- Room freezes for the finale: the background is held still, only the chat moves.
