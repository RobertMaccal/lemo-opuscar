VISUAL STYLE BIBLE: aiTrends 2D, cinematic ink stick figures, 9:16
==================================================================

FORMAT
- Aspect 9:16 vertical, logical stage 1080x1920, deliver 1080p.
- No letterbox bars (bars: none). Use thin "scope" bars only on the freeze
  moment (clip 12) if wanted.
- Safe area: faces, labels and diagrams inside the central ~900x1500.
  Burned-in captions sit at about 79% of the height. Ground line
  GY = 0.68 x H, so characters always stand above the captions.
- Captions ON, except on the title card (clip 3) and the end beat (clip 15),
  where on-screen text would duplicate them.
- Style preset: DUSK (closest cinematic-dark preset), then this bible.

THE LOOK IN ONE LINE
A stick-figure documentary lit like a film. Moody plum/navy rooms with one
warm practical light, and clean, bright blueprint anatomy whenever the
mechanism is explained. Clarity beats mood inside diagrams.

DIAGRAM COLOUR KEY (never changes meaning)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Blueprint blue (ui.blueprint): organ outlines, ducts, tubes.
- CREAM particles (#f4efe6): sperm cells (tiny tadpole dots, only ever tiny).
- AMBER/GOLD (ui.gold): seminal-vesicle fluid. Also gold for key numbers.
- TEAL (#1abc9c): prostate fluid.
- CORAL (ui.accent): warnings, failures, backward flow, "trap" moments.
- White pulse lines: nerve or hormone signals.

THE SIGNATURE MOTIF: "THE VOLUME CYLINDER"
A tall lab measuring cylinder (fits a 9:16 frame), labelled only at the top
"~1.5-5 mL TYPICAL" when first shown. It fills in layers that keep the same
order and colour all video:
  bottom cream sliver = sperm (about 2-5%)
  middle teal band = prostate (about 20-30%)
  top amber band = seminal vesicles (about 60-70%)
The layers are a compositional chart, not a claim about how semen settles
in real life. It appears in clips 2, 6, 7, 9 and the final recap (15), so
the viewer watches the answer build.

ANATOMY SCHEMATIC RULES (clinical, non-explicit)
- Simplified side-view (sagittal) pelvic schematic in blueprint line art,
  like a medical textbook. External genitalia reduced to a minimal neutral
  outline or cropped out of frame. Nothing sexual or graphic, ever.
- Correct relationships (stack vertically for 9:16):
  bladder at top centre; seminal vesicles BEHIND (posterior to) the bladder;
  prostate directly BELOW the bladder, wrapped around the urethra; testis and
  epididymis at the bottom; the vas deferens runs from the epididymis UP and
  over, behind the bladder, joins the seminal vesicle duct to form the
  ejaculatory duct, which enters the prostatic urethra.
- Flow arrows always point in the anatomical direction of transport.
  Backward arrows appear only in the coral "failure" inset.
- At most 1-3 labels per shot, Nunito Black, cream.
- Scale changes (zoom into a gland) use a visible circular magnifier ring.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Titles and big lines: Fredoka Bold, ALL CAPS, warm glow.
- Labels and numbers: Nunito Black. Key numbers in gold.

CAMERA AND EDIT GRAMMAR
- Every shot moves: push-ins (1.00 to 1.08), slow vertical drifts (natural
  in 9:16), pull-back reveals.
- Match cuts follow the flow: duct to gland to urethra, cylinder layer to
  cylinder layer.
- One freeze-frame signature moment (clip 12, the testosterone trap): flash
  in, cool tint, particles hanging, corner badge "TRAP".
- One giant-face close-up (bigFace) for the twist reaction (clip 1 end or
  clip 2 open).
- Transitions: mostly hard cuts; "zoom" into blueprint; "flash" into the
  freeze; "dip" before the final recap.

SOUND PALETTE (separate from narration; narration always wins)
Low synth pulse under the blueprint segments; soft glassy "blip" when labels
pop; liquid "pour" swells as cylinder layers fill; a muscle-ring "clunk" for
the bladder seal; a soft "error" buzz for the coral backflow; a clock tick
and page flips for the calendar; a freeze "whoomp" for the trap; cash-
register "ding" mocking the ad. Subtle music, ducked under the voice.

FORBIDDEN
No realism, 3D-looking characters, photos, emoji or clip-art. No explicit or
sexual imagery. No anatomy reversed or invented. No sperm drawn huge as if
they were the volume. No garment colour changes. No static shots. No walls
of text. No numbers on screen that aren't in the ledger. No brand names or
real product packaging (supplement bottles are generic, labelled only
"ZINC", "CoQ10", "ASHWAGANDHA").
