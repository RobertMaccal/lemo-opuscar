# Image-source and rights log

Rule for this film (STYLE §10 + your brief): only public domain, CC0 or CC BY material appear as evidence. Both layers are checked: **the artwork's** copyright and **the photograph's**. Nothing is generated to stand in for a real artwork or a real photograph. Drawn substitutes are line schematics tagged *schematic* on screen.

**Moving image:** the style normally wants a moving-image source. No auction footage or museum film is licensable here, so the film's sources are photographs, documents, data and measured schematics (STYLE §9: "Faking a multi-camera event…": the structure still holds). Movement comes from magnification, the drawn evidence and the readouts.
