**SEQ 2 · THE AGE OF PAINTING** (0:16–0:40)

> Van Gogh turned the view from his asylum window into a sky that moves. *(F6)*

**SEQ 3 · THE REVOLUTION OF 1917** (0:40–1:03)

> But the real break came twenty years before *Guernica*. *(F9, F10: 1917 vs 1937)*
> In 1917, Marcel Duchamp signed a urinal with a fake name, and sent it to an exhibition that promised to show everything. *(F10, F11)*
> They hid it behind a partition. *(F11)*
> [pause 1.0 s]

**SEQ 4 · WHEN THE IDEA BECAME ART** (1:03–1:24)

> In 1964, Andy Warhol showed boxes that looked just like supermarket cartons. *(F14)*
> A philosopher asked why those were art, and the ones in the stockroom weren't. His answer: an artworld. *(F15)*
> By the late sixties, art could be an instruction. An ordinary object. A question. *(F16)*

**SEQ 6 · THE BANANA RETURNS** (1:44–2:10)
