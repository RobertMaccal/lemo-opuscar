# Reference analysis: "The Job Nobody Wants in Japan" (720×1280, 30 fps, 60.5 s)

## Picture
- **2.5D build.** Real 3D sets (perspective rooms, desks, doors, ceilings with grid tiles) rendered flat-shaded with a thick black ink outline (~3–4 px at 720p, ≈5 px at 1080p) on every silhouette and hard edge. Surfaces are painted textures (brick, carpet, wood) with soft gradient lighting, coloured light pools from lamps, warm/cool contrast (deep ultramarine shadows, warm lamp yellow).
- **Characters are flat 2D cut-outs standing in the 3D sets** (billboards): huge round head (≈40% of body height), thick outline, simple hair shape as a cap (black, grey, brown), dot or ring eyes with tiny pupils, small mouth that changes shape (grin with tooth grid, open laugh, flat line, wobbly worry), sweat drop, bean/capsule torso (suit, shirt colour), thin stick limbs with ball hands, small shoes. Subtle rim/gradient shading on head and body.
- **Acting** is pose-to-pose with slow drift: head tilts, eye darts, blinks, a lean, a walk that is mostly a slide with a small bob. Props animate (hammer swing, gift box opens, clock hands).
- **Camera never stops.** Optical-flow measurement: no static frame in 60 s. Constant slow push-in (≈0.3–1 % scale per frame), plus truck/orbit moves with real parallax, low angles, overhead shots, POV shots past foreground objects. Big moves: fast dolly-in at the open (6 frames), whip pans (≈2–6 px/frame flow at 180 px width) at 6 s, 26 s, 29 s, 44 s.
- **Depth of field everywhere**: shallow focus, heavy background bokeh, focus pulls between foreground prop and character (0–3 s).
- **Transitions**: almost no hard cuts. 8–10 frame cross-dissolves between moving shots (both shots keep moving through the dissolve), occasional whip/zoom.
- **Shot length**: a new picture idea every 1.5–3 s, tied to the narration phrase.
- **Captions**: ONE word (sometimes two short ones) at a time, synced to the voice, white heavy rounded sans with dark stroke and soft shadow, centred horizontally at ≈70 % of frame height, ~44 px at 720p (≈66 px at 1080p). No caption box.
- Light grade: soft bloom on lamps, gentle vignette, slight softness/grain.

## Sound
- Narrator: fast, conversational, ≈3.7 words/s, punchy pauses (0.25–0.7 s) after key lines.
- Music bed is loud (only 2–6 dB below voice), continuous; it **cuts out completely** at two dramatic beats (≈9 s, ≈38 s) and comes back.
- SFX: full-band whooshes on every camera whip/dissolve, small pops/clicks on prop and face changes, ticks on the clock, impact hits on button presses.

## What we carry over (with a horror palette)
3D ink-outlined sets + 2D round-head characters; constant camera motion with DOF; dissolves; one-word captions at 70 % height; dense SFX on every visual change; music that drops to silence on the biggest beats.
