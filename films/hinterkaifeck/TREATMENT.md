# The Footprints — treatment

Case: the Hinterkaifeck murders, Bavaria, 31 March 1922 (facts checked against Wikipedia: footprints from the forest with none leading back, attic footsteps, missing key, Munich newspaper, maid quit ~6 months earlier, new maid arrived that day, four lured to the barn one by one, Josef in his bassinet, killer stayed ~3 days: fed cattle, ate all bread, cut meat, neighbour saw oven smoke "with a revolting smell", found 4 April, >100 suspects, never solved, mattock found hidden in the attic at demolition).

## Three structures
1. **Chronological** — farm, family, omens, night, discovery, investigation. Safe, but the hook comes late.
2. **Discovery-first** — open on neighbours finding the barn, then flash back. Strong, but the grisly image is the hook (not Instagram-safe, and less eerie).
3. **Omen-first loop (chosen)** — open on the single most uncanny fact (footprints in, none out), then pay it off step by step and close on the attic: the weapon was hidden where the footsteps came from. Every ~8 s a new escalation (maid quits → key → newspaper → footsteps → nobody there → the night → "the killer didn't leave" → smoke → never found). The middle has a second hook ("here's the scariest part").

## Look
The reference's grammar (3D ink-outlined sets, 2D round-head cut-out people, always-moving camera with deep DOF, 8–10-frame dissolves, one-word captions at 70 % height) in a horror palette: moonlit ultramarine snow and night, warm lantern/oil-lamp amber pools, one blood-red accent (the barn door, the newspaper masthead, the calendar). Falling snow, breath fog, drifting dust in light beams, bloom on flames.

The killer is never shown: only footprints, a shadow, a figure-shaped darkness at the forest edge. Violence is never on screen: doors close, lanterns go out, the camera turns away.

## Sound
Low drone + detuned music box + heartbeat score; music drops to silence on "Nobody was there." and "The killer didn't leave." Every visual change gets a sound: crunching snow, wind, creaking beams, footsteps overhead, lantern hiss, barn door, clock, cattle, fire crackle, stingers on the hooks.
