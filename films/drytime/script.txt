[serious] It happened at a psychiatric hospital, on a quiet, sunny afternoon.

A patient named Harold was walking along the edge of the swimming pool, when he slipped… and fell into the deep end. Harold couldn't swim.

Before anyone could react, another patient, Walter, jumped in after him. He pulled Harold out of the water… and dragged him to safety.

The head psychiatrist, Doctor Bennett, saw the whole thing from his window. The next morning, he called Walter into his office.

[warmly] "Walter," he said, "for years we've tried to help you. But yesterday, you risked your own life to save someone else. That is not the act of a sick man. I think you're healed. Congratulations… you can go home."

Walter smiled. "Thank you very much, Doctor."

The doctor shook his hand, and asked one last question. "By the way… where is Harold? The man you saved?"

[casually] "Oh, him? I hung him up on the balcony… to dry."
