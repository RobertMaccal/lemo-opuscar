How the film words it, to stay accurate:

- "inspired by a little boy… catching bugs" — Tajiri's own answer in TIME 1999; the film also shows the link cable and the team, so it does not claim the hobby was the only source.
- The beetle on the leaf is a symbol of the hobby, not a specific historical beetle.
- The creature crossing the cable is drawn as an imagined vision (glow, sparkle), not as real hardware behaviour.
- "Together with his team at Game Freak" — the studio scene shows several developers; Tajiri is never shown making the game alone.
- No dialogue is invented; nobody speaks on screen.
- Card prices: past sales only, with their year; the future is framed as unknown ("Nobody knows. Prices can crash just as fast as they soar.").
