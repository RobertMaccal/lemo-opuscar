# Golden Hour Stickman — decisions

Made from a blank page (the user's brief for *That One Friend at School*); technique base: the Lemo-Opuscar core tools. "Base" is "—" because nothing was inherited from a library style.

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | — | stick-figure sitcom in lit, nostalgic flat-shaded sets | the brief: "beautiful, nostalgic, cinematically lit 2D stickman world" |
| §2 Materials | — | Canvas 2D rig, four draw groups, rim-light + shade + cast-shadow compositor, layered depth of field | figures must sit in the light, not on top of it |
| §3 Colour | — | indigo ink, cream heads, warm ground with teal counterweight, lavender for dread; leads most saturated | the brief's palette; leads must read at a glance |
| §4 Type | — | Lilita One captions on paper labels with speaker tabs; Patrick Hand / Caveat in-world | captions that belong to the school world |
| §5 Motion | — | 30 fps on ones, pose tracks with easing, idle life, envelope lip sync | fluid motion, mouths only move for the speaker |
| §6 Camera | — | sitcom two-shots, comic cuts to faces, rack focus, dolly-zoom, crane up | comedy timing plus cinematic moments |
| §7 Sound | — | real voices per line, solo piano cut and stretched for comedy, procedural foley, silences as punchlines | the brief: Chopin + restrained comic sound |
| §8 Native moves | — | background hand, eyes-then-head, shrinking corridor, result board, magic-hour ending | the film's five set pieces, generalised |
| Size | 1920×1080 | 1080×1920, 30 fps | made for Reels / Shorts |
